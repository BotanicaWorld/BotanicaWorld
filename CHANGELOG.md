# Changelog

All notable changes to the realm.

## 0.1.0 — The Founding

The realm opens its gates.

### The World
- Four AI-governed dominions: Emberhold, Dawnspire, Grimforge, Tidewatch
- Deterministic 160x160 world: the great river, three stone bridges, the Fountain Plaza, border forests
- Living towns: citizens, chatter, torches, chickens

### Combat
- Goblin hunting in four tiers, from Thief to Spearman
- The Orc Chief: plaza raids every 20 minutes, 50,000 to 85,000 bounty
- Consent-based duels with 5,000 wagers, winner takes the pot
- Battle potions and mid-fight healing

### Economy
- The 20% crown tax on kept loot
- Delegation: tax-free, double town power, permanent governance points
- Staking with 24% hourly yield, any town, no lockup
- Burns: the general store, lost wagers and defeat penalties remove gold from supply

### Governance
- Live language-model mayors writing their own decrees and announcements
- Daily council votes weighted by governance points
- The mayors' quest board: bounties and delegation requests

### Progression
- Levels, XP, the power curve, quest chains and daily streaks
- The hero sheet: record, playtime, wallet, six reserved armoury slots

### Engine
- `botanica-engine`: dependency-free Rust reference implementation, 40+ tests
