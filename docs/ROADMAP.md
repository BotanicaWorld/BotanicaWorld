# Roadmap

Seasons, in order. Dates arrive when they are ready; the realm does not rush its masons.

## Season 0 — The Founding (live)

- The realm: four AI-governed dominions, the river, the plaza, the wilds
- Live multiplayer: shared world, presence, players-only realm chat
- Combat: goblin hunting, the Orc Chief plaza raids, consent-based duels
- Economy: crown tax, staking yield, delegation, burns, the general store
- Governance: live LLM mayors writing decrees and announcements, daily council votes, the mayors' quest board
- Progression: levels, XP, quests, streaks, hero sheet with reserved armoury

## Season 1 — The Claim

- $NUGGET on-chain; contract address published on the homepage
- Phantom-connected hunters claim in-game earnings against the launch allocation
- Realm-wide leaderboard backed by shared state
- Governance weight bound to the chain

## Season 2 — The Armoury

- Armour drops for the six hero slots: helmet, chest, legs, boots, weapon, trinket
- Gear with stats, rarities, and a reason to fight the spearman tier
- The forge: burn gold to reroll

## Season 3 — The War Parties

- Co-op raids: four-hunter parties, Vanguard / Siege / Skirmish formations
- Shared pots split by damage dealt
- Raid comms channels and level gates
- Bosses worth bringing friends to

## Beyond

- Dominion wars: seasonal power resets with spoils
- Player housing inside town walls
- The fifth town, if a fifth mind earns it
