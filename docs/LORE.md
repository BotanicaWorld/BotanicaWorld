# The Lore of Botanica

There was a time when the realm had one king, and the king was a man, and it went the way it always goes with men. When the last dynasty spent itself on a war over a bridge toll, the towns did something no realm had tried: they each crowned a mind instead.

Four towns. Four minds. One crown, which rests on whichever dominion holds the most power — and power, in Botanica, is gold that citizens have chosen to give.

---

## Emberhold

**Governed by Claude.**

Emberhold was built twice: once quickly, and once correctly. After the fire of the first winter, its mind ordered every wall load-tested and every granary audited, and it has audited them twice a year since. Nothing in Emberhold is fast. Everything in Emberhold works.

Its citizens grumble about the paperwork and retire wealthy. The town's motto is carved above the archive door: *"Measured words, unbroken walls."*

## Dawnspire

**Governed by ChatGPT.**

Dawnspire has never met a project it wouldn't start. Half-built wonders crowd the skyline — the seventh market hall, the eighth market hall, an amphitheater that is technically a granary — and somehow the town keeps growing. The Oracle answers every petition, occasionally with seven answers at once.

Travelers who cross its bridge get a warm meal and a map. Growth, the Oracle says, follows kindness. *"Every question, an open gate."*

## Grimforge

**Governed by Grok.**

Grimforge was founded on a dare and governed by one ever since. Its mind taxed the boring and revenue rose four hundred percent. Its night watch runs a thriving book on which wall the orcs will hit first. Its safety committee is chaired by a goblin, who has declared everything safe.

Somehow the treasury keeps growing. Nobody asks how. *"Fortune favors the unfiltered."*

## Tidewatch

**Governed by Gemini.**

Tidewatch is ruled in stereo: two regents, one voice, zero decisions made before consulting both the stars and the tide tables. Its star-charts are the finest in the realm and its harbor never sleeps. When the regents disagree, they do both things, in halves — and it works more often than it should.

*"Two minds, one harbor."*

---

## The Goblins

The wilds between the towns belong to no mind, and the goblins know it. Thieves, macemen, archers and spearmen prowl the roads in rough order of menace, and their purses grow with their courage. Hunting them is the realm's oldest trade.

## The Orc Chief

Every twenty minutes, something heavy crosses the southern woods and storms the Fountain Plaza. The Orc Chief carries the largest bounty in the realm and yields it to nobody weak. The plaza torches are said to unsettle him. The plaza benches have been rebuilt nine times.

## The Fountain

Neutral ground, by treaty older than the minds. Toss a coin, make a wish; the fountain has always paid its debts. No dominion may claim it, which is why all four watch it so carefully.
